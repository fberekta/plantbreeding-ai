 🌱 PlantBreeding AI

 AI Assistant for Plant Breeding Research

PlantBreeding AI is a simple AI-powered research assistant designed for students and researchers in plant breeding.

The project helps users explore crop traits, identify breeding goals, and generate ideas for further research.

 🎯 Problem

Plant breeding students often need to analyse many traits and scientific sources before planning an experiment. This can be time-consuming, especially when organising information and deciding what to investigate next.

 💡 Solution

PlantBreeding AI provides a simple interface for exploring plant traits and generating research recommendations based on a selected crop and breeding goal.

 ✨ Features

 🌱 Select a crop
 🎯 Select a breeding goal
 🧬 Explore important plant traits
 📊 Analyse example plant data
 💡 Generate research recommendations
 🔬 Suggest possible experimental directions

🧪 Example
Crop: Soybean
Breeding goal: Drought resistance
Important traits:

  Plant height
  Root development
  Yield
  Flowering time
  Seed weight

Possible research direction:

Compare soybean lines with different drought-response traits and investigate which traits are associated with better performance under water stress.
